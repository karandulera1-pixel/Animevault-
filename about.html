About AnimeVault

What is AnimeVault?

AnimeVault is an independent website for anime-inspired wallpapers and unofficial fan creations. Some wallpapers may be AI-generated fan art featuring characters from existing anime, manga, or other entertainment franchises.

Important: AnimeVault is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the owners of the anime and manga franchises represented in fan-art wallpapers.

Fan art

Characters, names, logos, and franchise elements belong to their respective rights holders. AnimeVault does not claim ownership of those characters or franchises.

Original AnimeVault work

AnimeVault may also publish original characters and original scenes created specifically for the site. Those works are presented separately from franchise fan art.
